Frequently Asked Questions

What surfaces do your restoration kits work on?

Our restoration compounds and polishes are specifically formulated for marine gelcoat and fiberglass surfaces. They're effective on boats, yachts, jet skis, and other marine vessels. The complete system handles everything from heavy oxidation removal to final high-gloss finishing.

How long does the sealant protection last?

Our marine sealants provide durable protection that can last 6-12 months depending on environmental conditions and maintenance. In harsh saltwater environments with proper application, you can expect reliable UV protection and hydrophobic performance throughout the boating season.
