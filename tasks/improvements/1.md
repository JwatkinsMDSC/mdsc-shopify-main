## Optional Improvements (NOT required before Phase 2)

These are *nice-to-have*, not blockers.

1. **Centralize magic strings**

   * `"In Stock"`, `"Out of Stock"`, `"Adding..."`
     Consider a future `window.B2B.copy` or config object.

2. **Variant availability nuance**

   * Shopify allows “continue selling when out of stock”
   * You already left room for this (`available` vs `inventoryQuantity`)
   * Good candidate for a future enhancement, not needed now.

3. **Controller ordering**

   * If future controllers depend on others, you may later want an explicit init order.
   * Current generic loop is fine for Phase 1.
